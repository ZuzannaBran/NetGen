NetGen Data Storage Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-05

1. Data Controller

Zuzanna Baran

Email: baran_zuzanna@outlook.com

Jurisdiction: Poland (EU GDPR)

2. Scope

This policy describes how NetGen stores and processes data for:

  • Account authentication (Firebase Authentication)
  • Contact data storage (Firebase Firestore)
  • Application hosting (Firebase Hosting)
  • Feedback delivery (EmailJS and Firebase Functions, optional SendGrid)
  • Operational logs (Firebase/Google Cloud service logs)

3. Data We Store

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Contacts: names, surnames, notes, tags, event/source info, and contact methods you enter.
  • Feedback messages you send to the creator (if you use the feedback form).
  • Technical logs: timestamps, request metadata, and error logs generated by Firebase services.

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Provide login and account management.
  • Store, retrieve, and synchronize your contacts across devices.
  • Enable optional feedback delivery to the creator.
  • Maintain service reliability and security.

5. Legal Basis

Processing is based on your consent and the necessity to provide the NetGen service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(b)).

6. Data Processors and Transfers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Hosting, Functions).
  • EmailJS for client-side email delivery (if enabled).
  • SendGrid via Firebase Functions (if configured).

Data may be processed on infrastructure operated by these providers.

7. Retention

Data is stored until you delete it or request account removal. Operational logs may be retained for security and reliability purposes for a limited time.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent at any time by contacting the data controller.

9. Security

NetGen uses Firebase security rules and authenticated access to protect stored data. No system can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures are applied.

10. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact: baran_zuzanna@outlook.com.
